Packaging Sector

Specialist high-speed packaging line automation, filling line retrofits, conveyor synchronization, and drive replacements for UK packaging plants.

Maximising Line Efficiency & Throughput in Packaging Environments

In continuous packaging lines, downtime is directly measured in lost units and heavy revenue impact. Smart Flow Engineering provides expert electrical, control, and drive engineering to ensure packaging, labelling, cartoning, and palletising machinery operates at peak speed, reliability, and precision.

High-Speed Line Precision

Drive positioning & PLC synchronisation for high-volume packaging

Our Packaging Engineering Capabilities

  • Conveyor & Line Synchronisation - Multi-axis VFD speed matching and tension control
  • Filling & Labelling Automation - Precision timing, photoelectric sensor & PLC integration
  • Drive Retrofits & Modernisation - Replacement of obsolete AC/DC drives with modern energy-efficient VFDs
  • Robotic Palletising & Wrapping - Safety interlock integration, RAMS compliance & panel wiring
  • Obsolescence Management - Upgrading end-of-life PLCs and HMIs without extended line stoppage
  • Planned Fault Investigation & Shutdown Support - Emergency fault diagnostics for high-speed packaging lines
  • Safety System Guarding & Interlocking - ISO 13849 safety circuit verification & compliance


Tailored Engineering for High-Volume Packaging Lines

Whether you operate automated case packers, shrink wrappers, filling carousels, or high-speed belt conveyors, Smart Flow Engineering integrates state-of-the-art controls with existing equipment, extending asset life and eliminating bottle-necks.
