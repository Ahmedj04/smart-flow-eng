Industrial Drives & Motor Control

Assessment, supply, replacement, retrofit and commissioning of industrial drive systems.

Drives • Controls • Electrical

Industrial Drive Assessment, Retrofit & Supply

Smart Flow Engineering helps manufacturers reduce downtime by assessing, replacing and modernising ageing AC variable speed drives (VFDs) and DC drives. We evaluate drive condition, age, reliability, and supportability to resolve component obsolescence before failures stop production.

Sprint Electric Technology Training

Engineers trained on Sprint Electric technology can support relevant drive applications within an agreed scope. Training does not represent a completed Smart Flow project reference.

Key Drive Capabilities

  • Drive Condition & Reliability Audits - Evaluating drive health, thermal status, and spares availability
  • AC & DC Drive Retrofits - Modernising legacy drives with minimal mechanical/electrical disruption
  • Obsolescence Replacement - Eliminating unsupportable drive hardware before failures occur
  • Motor Control Modernisation - Motor starters, soft starters, contactors, and MCC integration
  • Precision Drive Tuning - Speed, torque, acceleration ramps, and fieldbus setup
  • Drive Fault Investigation - Diagnosis of drive faults within agreed support arrangements

The Product → Engineering Difference
