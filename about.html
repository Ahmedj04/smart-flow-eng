About Smart Flow Engineering

Smart Flow Engineering supports system integrators, OEMs, contractors and industrial operators with electrical installation, automation integration, drives and control upgrades, testing and commissioning.

Specialist Engineering Delivery

Smart Flow Engineering Ltd is a specialist industrial electrical and automation engineering delivery business. (Pearlcon is our parent company.)

Who We Are

Smart Flow Engineering supports system integrators, OEMs, contractors and industrial operators with electrical installation, automation integration, drives and control upgrades, testing and commissioning.

We hold Achilles BuildingConfidence registration and SafeContractor approval. We support Tier 1 contractors, OEMs, system integrators and industrial operators with defined work packages.

Our technical focus is Drives • Controls • Electrical: site wiring, drive and control upgrades, equipment interfaces, I/O testing and commissioning support.

Our Structure

Pearlcon is our parent company. Its leadership experience and support are distinct from Smart Flow’s operating history; Pearlcon projects are not Smart Flow project references.

Our Vision

To be a trusted engineering delivery partner recognized across the UK infrastructure and industrial sectors for intelligent automation, electrical, and drive solutions — enabling clients to operate safer, smarter, and more sustainably while supporting the transition to digitally connected and energy-efficient assets.
